## Verdict: v10 is not promoted

`activeVersion` stays `v8`. Recall fell 2.8 points at the median and the
critical count rose by 4; 26 required items regressed against 15 improved. The
sign test (p = 0.117) says that is not distinguishable from noise, which is not
a defence — "no worse than v8" is not a reason to ship a prompt, and the point
estimate points the wrong way.

**The regressions are not near the rule.** `dom:preference/vegan` (60% → 10%),
`dom:trait/social` (90% → 40%), `group-ramble:loop/cook` (60% → 10%) have
nothing to do with distributing group facts. That is the signature of attention
dilution rather than a rule interacting badly with a neighbour: rule 38 is ~30
lines of sub-bullets appended to a prompt that was already the longest it has
ever been.

## What this run did NOT measure

**The benefit.** `plural-attribution` was not collected — see the manifest's
`memos` list. So this is a measurement of rule 38's *cost* with no evidence
whatsoever about whether it does the thing it was written to do.

The cause is structural, not an oversight in this run. `measure --live`
enumerates `docs/evals/fixtures/*.json` and reads each fixture's `source` to
find its transcript, so **the corpus it collects is the set of memos that
already have fixtures**. A golden authored ahead of its fixture can therefore
never be measured, and `measure.py` prints exactly the opposite —
"measured once a live extraction produces one — `orbit-evals measure --live`" —
which is false for every golden in that state. `tag-discipline` has been sitting
there since 2026-08-07 for the same reason.

Recorded as FN-49. Until it is fixed, "goldens awaiting a fixture" names a queue
that nothing drains.
