## The instrument, not the prompt

v10 and v11 produced the **identical** split against the same baseline — 15
improved, 26 regressed, 48 unchanged, p = 0.117 — while completely different
items moved. v10 added 494 words; v11 added 56. Two very different edits cannot
plausibly produce the same effect size; that split is what comparing any two
collections of this corpus looks like.

The item-level evidence is decisive. `homonym:person:sarah_o` across six
collections:

| v6 | v7 | v8 | v9 | v10 | v11 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40% | 30% | 70% | 10% | 80% | 10% |

**v10 contains v11's rule plus 440 more words**, and scores 80% where v11 scores
10% and v8 scores 70%. A monotone cause cannot produce that ordering. The item
is a coin flip, and it was the headline regression in this comparison.

Across the 89 required items present in all six collections:

- **24%** swing ≥ 60 points between collections
- **39%** swing ≥ 40 points
- median swing: **20 points**

Some of that spread is real — v9's halved prompt is legibly worse almost
everywhere, and the hedge items climb when v7 and v8 add hedge rules. The metric
resolves *large* prompt changes. It does not resolve a 56-word rule, and a
15-vs-26 item split sits comfortably inside what it produces from noise alone.

## What this measurement does and does not support

**Supports:** rule 38 does the thing it was written to do.
`plural-attribution:theo/education` — the distributed half, the Harvard case —
goes **60% → 100%**, with 0 of 17 items regressing on those memos. That is the
most specific result in this set, and it is the only one measured against a
baseline collected for the purpose.

**Does not support:** any claim about v11's cost, in either direction. "26
regressed" is not evidence of harm here, and neither would "26 improved" have
been evidence of benefit. At this resolution the shared-corpus comparison is
uninformative for a change this small.

**Retracts:** the v10 verdict's reasoning, not its outcome. That entry read the
regressions as attention dilution from a 30-line rule. Those same items swing as
much between collections that share a prompt lineage, and v10's delta was
additionally confounded by 142 words of *versioning prose in the file header*,
which `ExtractionPrompt.system()` sends to the model as instructions (FN-50).
v10 remains unpromoted — but "it cost recall" was never established.
