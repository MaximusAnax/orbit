Orbit — the capture flow, in both rooms

The daily loop in the ratified language: Home → Capture → Transcript → Review → Saved, every screen shown by day and by night. Capture follows life, not the clock — a breakfast, a run, a late dinner — so neither room is the flow's default; the app simply follows the system setting. Day content is the real Nikos picnic memo; night content is the real Futureforce dinner memo. New decisions the flow forced are flagged at the bottom.

New decisions this flow forced (to fold into DESIGN.md on ratification)

Buttons. Primary = ember fill with a new token ember-ink (near-white by day, deep umber by night — amber needs dark text). Secondary = ghost outline on pill-edge. Tertiary = underlined text in ink-faint ("Later"). Three tiers, no more.

The two-voices law bites on screen titles. "Does this look right?" is the system talking, so titles are sans/650 — the serif headers in the old Warm Archive prototype violated the law we later ratified. Serif appears in this flow only where life does: transcripts, quotes, names.

Uncertainty inherits the note material. Ask-cards use the sticky-note tokens plus an ember border — questions sit on the same material as the owe-sticky: things to handle by hand, in both rooms.

Home: capture-first, three quiet zones

Search ("Who…?") on top as a pill; the mic as the room's single large ember object with its halo doubling as the tap target; below, only two quiet things — set-aside captures (count + "no rush", never a badge) and "Lately", a row of recent portraits as pure navigation. No feed, no stats, no suggestions: Principle 8 says better relationships, not more activity, and a home screen is where engagement mechanics usually creep in.

The privacy line lives on the capture screen ("Recorded and transcribed on this device") — quiet, permanent, load-bearing for trust.
