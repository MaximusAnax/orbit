Orbit — home & search, redesigned against the inventory

Home is now literally the three doors from ORBIT.md §18: capture in the center, one omnisearch field, and timely context that opens briefs. "Lately" is gone (no inventoried job), set-asides demoted to a footer line (rare-tier). Below it: the search-results surface — door two — designed for the first time, in three query shapes.

Home: what changed and why

"Lately" is cut. It served no inventoried use case — generic recent-items UI that snuck in. Set-asides demoted from a prime card to a footer line: triage is rare-tier, and prime position implied debt.

"Today" is the new zone — at most two items, only when genuinely timely (a life event in its window, a thread reaching its expected resolution), each carrying its reason and opening the person's brief. It is Principle 9 as a surface: context first, person second, and never a bare "message Sarah." When nothing is timely the zone collapses entirely — no filler, no streak. This is maintenance-as-context, not a maintenance door.

The search placeholder teaches the door: it rotates through real query shapes — a name, a company, a fragment — so the field advertises that all three are the same box.

Search: one door, three query shapes

Name resolves people directly — with the how-you-know line as the identity anchor, because "which Sarah?" is answered by provenance, not surname.

Question ("who do I know at Google?") returns an answer, not matches: firsthand people first with time-bounded evidence, then secondhand paths — known-of people clearly marked, each citing who mentioned them. The warm-path use case lives in that second band.

Fragment ("greece picnic guy") matches on evidence, and the result shows why it matched with the matching facts underlined — tip-of-the-tongue resolved by showing your own memory back to you. Latency bar set by the mid-conversation check: results render as you type, no search button anywhere.
